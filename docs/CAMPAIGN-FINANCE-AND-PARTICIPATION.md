## Named contributions

Luke's policy is to publish every accepted donation by name, amount or non-cash value, date and type, including below mandatory disclosure thresholds. Donors must understand and agree to this condition before acceptance. Donation enquiries and pledges are private until an actual contribution is accepted and reviewed for publication. Volunteer names are not automatically published.

The public register excludes addresses, contact information, banking details, payment and receipt references, eligibility evidence and private notes. Required statutory disclosures are separate. Ordinary volunteer labour and incidental use of a volunteer's vehicle or equipment are distinct from gifts under ECQ's guidance.

## Source and review

The private command centre's Budget and reporting section holds contributions, budget options and expenses. It separates pledges, receipts awaiting review, accepted cash, non-cash value, incurred costs and paid costs. Its totals describe recorded entries rather than a reconciled bank balance. Opening an older local store adds empty finance collections in memory without changing the existing file or inventing records.

An operator explicitly reviews entries for public reporting. Preparing a public draft selects only allowed fields and refuses to omit an accepted donation awaiting named-publication review. The draft does not automatically publish or submit an ECQ return. After reviewing its completeness and accuracy, replace `data/campaign-finance-public.json` with the approved public snapshot and run `npm run check`. Review the generated reporting page and the source diff before the normal publication step. Never copy the private ledger itself.

The initial public register is intentionally unpopulated. It does not assert zero income, zero spending or a reconciled balance. Public CSV files and the public JSON snapshot contain the same selected records as the reporting page.
