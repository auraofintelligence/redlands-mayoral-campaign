# Media directory notes

Checked: 8 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane.

This is a browsable set of public media options for Luke. It does not prescribe an outreach sequence, daily routine or ranking. Nothing in the directory has been contacted, subscribed to or sent a campaign message by this research work.

## What is included

The initial directory contains 44 entries across three files: 25 print, television, online and commentary entries; 15 radio entries; and four public organisation, forum or official-source entries. Of the 25 print and online entries, nine are named professional journalists, editors or a podcast host associated with outlets already listed. Those nine are additional ways to understand an outlet's published work, not nine extra media organisations.

The remaining 35 directory entries include outlets, programmes, channels and organisations. They are not 35 independent owners. ABC news and radio services share a broadcaster; SBS News and SBS Audio share a broadcaster. The Redland City Minute's Substack is recorded as a source link on its existing entry, rather than counted as another outlet.

Local sources cover mainland Redlands, the Bay Islands and Bayside. Broader Queensland and national options are included with their actual area stated. Westender is chiefly a West End publication with historical Redland reporting; it is not presented as a Redland local newspaper.

## What the evidence labels mean

- **election-coverage-verified** means a linked, dated source was located concerning the current mayoral contest, the vacancy that triggered it, or election arrangements. It does not mean the outlet has covered Luke. The print and online file has 14 such entries, including repeated source articles attached to their named authors.
- **outlet-verified** means the publisher, programme or professional affiliation is supported by a primary source. It does not establish current by-election coverage. The print and online file has 11 such entries.
- Coverage dates describe the publication, not a future commitment. Some display titles are shortened descriptions; the linked source supplies the original headline.

The 5 November Minute report includes an unconfirmed-date statement superseded by the ECQ announcement that day. Earlier coverage of the proposed postal ballot is historical evidence of reporting, not the final election timetable. The [ECQ's 2026 announcements](https://www.ecq.qld.gov.au/media/2026-media-statements) provide the official source recorded in the campaign configuration.

## Contact routes and limits

Contact links come from the outlet, publisher, broadcaster or professional profile. They are public routes only. No personal phone numbers, private addresses, inferred email addresses, scraped group membership or political preference profiles are stored.

General publisher routes are labelled: The Courier-Mail uses [News Corp's enquiry form](https://www.newscorpaustralia.com/contact-us/), 10 News uses [Network 10's station contact page](https://10.com.au/contact-us), and 9News uses [Nine's published story-submission route](https://www.nine.com.au/contact-us). These are not promises of a direct response from a named journalist. Page access failures are recorded in individual notes rather than hidden.

This is not an exhaustive census. Paywalls, inaccessible pages, unindexed social posts and changing programme lineups limit discovery. No additional local podcast is labelled an election commentator without evidence. Current online publishing, an older local article and a contact page each establish different facts.

Listing does not imply political support, endorsement, willingness to publish, consent to recurring contact or priority over another option. There are no persuasion scores, voter segments or automated sends. Source links and notes let Luke choose what to read or explore next.
