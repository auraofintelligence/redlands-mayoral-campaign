# 38 Community venues and neighbourhood initiatives

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Local economy and environment

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Community venues and neighbourhood activities could offer more affordable ways to take part and make good use of the facilities already available.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Divisional councillors, existing organisers and willing venues could consider club, sport, outdoor learning, screenings and neighbourhood events together. Each community would help identify what suits its place and people.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Community Club Builder: Sandy Sports](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/community-club-builder-sandy-sports/)**. A practical playbook for forming and running a community club, using a Minjerribah sand-sports club as its worked example. It covers governance, inclusive leadership, membership, shared administration, AI assistance and funding preparation.

- **[Amity Outdoor Fitness and Safer Access](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/amity-outdoor-fitness-grant/)**. An Amity Point outdoor-activity-park proposal combining fitness equipment with safer Claytons Road access. Its planning pages cover crossing and traffic questions, parking edges, water access, activity routines, budgets, delivery and grant evidence.

- **[Straddie Headline Open Mic](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-headline-open-mic/)**. A daily board of funny English-language news headlines paired with planning for a possible Straddie comedy open-mic night. It offers a searchable headline list, dated archives, feed-selection notes and an open-mic format.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could work with divisional councillors and existing organisers on council venue access, leases, community programs and transparent support for willing neighbourhood hosts.

## Who else makes decisions

Each club, venue owner and community organiser chooses its participation, governance, activities and use of its own assets.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Community groups, co-operatives, schools, businesses and external funders as appropriate.

## How related interests would be handled

The proposal calls for visible venue, lease, grant and access criteria, with independent handling of any council arrangement benefiting Luke or an organisation he establishes or joins.
