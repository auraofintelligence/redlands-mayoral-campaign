# 15 Co-operative access to health and wellbeing tools

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Health, care and inclusion

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Members could share access to health and wellbeing equipment, spreading costs and agreeing fair arrangements for its use and upkeep.

## Words and project names

- **Community Hours (C-hours)**: A way to recognise verified voluntary human contribution and its outcomes. The source introduction describes one verified hour of contribution with no monetary equivalent. Participation and information sharing are choices.
- **Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT)**: Breathing oxygen in a pressurised chamber. Clinical services and proposed research have their own providers, participation choices and responsibilities.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Members and providers could agree how shared wellbeing equipment is owned, booked, paid for and maintained. Qualified providers would remain responsible for clinical services, alongside each person’s care choices.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Aura Genesis](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis/)**. A website presenting the Aura Genesis journey as sixty sessions combining reflection, health, personal digital twins and community participation. - **[Oceania Healthy De-Slop Co-ops](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Oceania-healthy-de-slop-co-ops/)**. A regional co-operative proposal for affordable wellbeing spaces, self-reflection, local computing and personal data stewardship. ## How this could move forward

Personal initiative with possible council involvement

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could bring community members, providers and co-operative advisers together around shared access. Council could consider relevant public-benefit support through its normal processes.

## Who else makes decisions

Members and any organisation they establish govern ownership and access. Providers retain their clinical and operating responsibilities, and relevant regulators retain their powers.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Community members, health/wellbeing providers, co-operative advisers, clinicians and regulators for the relevant tools.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke’s equipment, provider role or membership interest would be identified in any council-funded shared-access arrangement involving him.
