# 32 Sandworm underground-road feasibility

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Infrastructure, energy and coasts

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

The proposal examines underground roads on Minjerribah as a way to improve access and reduce impacts at the surface.

## What this could mean for Redlands

An options study for underground roads on Minjerribah would compare routes and requirements with surface improvements, public transport and options involving less new infrastructure.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Sandworm Subterranean Systems](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/sandworm-subterranean-systems/)**. A subterranean-systems research workbench exploring autonomous transport arteries and continuous town connections on Minjerribah. Its questions include tunnelling, erosion, spoil reuse, geopolymer materials, artificial reefs, wave energy and sand heat storage.

- **[Civilisation of Sand](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/civilisation-of-sand/)**. A subterranean Kardashev-scale city simulation, quest map and builder suite. It connects in-situ resources, material systems, AI and robotics, digital twins, care, consent and Joyful Responsible Abundance.

## How this could move forward

Council infrastructure and decisions by other authorities

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could seek a council position and scoped engineering work on Minjerribah road access, council road responsibilities and land interfaces, and convene the responsible landholders and transport authorities.

## Who else makes decisions

The relevant road and land owners, Traditional Owners and state/environmental authorities retain their decisions. Technical delivery and permissions depend on the particular alignment.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Council roads/planning teams, landholders, Traditional Owners, geotechnical/tunnelling engineers, universities and regulators.

## How related interests would be handled

Any council-funded road study, design licence, land arrangement or tunnelling contract involving Luke's Sandworm intellectual property, business or future delivery interest would require independent handling.
