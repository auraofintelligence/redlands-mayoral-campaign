# 32 Sandworm underground-road feasibility

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Infrastructure, energy and coasts

## Purpose

Test whether underground options could reduce surface impacts and improve access.

## Possible Redlands action

Seek a scoped options study for Minjerribah underground roads, compared with surface improvements, public transport and doing less infrastructure.

## Decision route

Council infrastructure and external approvals

## Mayoral and council lever

Seek a council position and scoped engineering work on Minjerribah road access, council road responsibilities and land interfaces; convene the responsible landholders and transport authorities.

## Other decision-makers

The relevant road and land owners, Traditional Owners and state/environmental authorities retain their decisions. Technical delivery and permissions depend on the particular alignment.

## Partners

Council roads/planning teams, landholders, Traditional Owners, geotechnical/tunnelling engineers, universities and regulators.

## Specific interest or transaction boundary

Independently handle any council-funded road study, design licence, land arrangement or tunnelling contract involving Luke’s Sandworm IP, business or future delivery interest.
