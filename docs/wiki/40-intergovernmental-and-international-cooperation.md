# 40 Intergovernmental and international cooperation

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Advocacy and research context

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Redlands could exchange research, teaching and practical knowledge with other communities and institutions, with each participant keeping authority over its own choices.

## Words and project names

- **Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth (GAJRA Earth)**: Luke’s proposed network connecting contribution, learning, events, travel, research and shared capability. A Redlands organisation would have its own members and agreed rules.

## What this could mean for Redlands

The diversification, Fiji–Australia Vuvale, GAJRA and long-term civilisation papers could inform invitations to exchange research, teaching and practical knowledge. Each invitation would identify the local project, each participant’s contribution, rights over information and creative or technical work, and the government or institution responsible.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[A Fair Go in the Age of Intelligence](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/UNGA81-Luke-Hayes/)**. Luke’s independent UNGA81 contribution, A Fair Go in the Age of Intelligence. It connects Joyful Responsible Abundance with shared capability, livelihoods and time, personal and civic intelligence, and Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth (GAJRA Earth).

- **[AUKUS Space Gambit](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aukus-space-gambit/)**. A research hypothesis exploring preservation, reboosting or repurposing of the International Space Station before deorbit. The site brings together the baseline, the proposed gambit, review questions, a source trail and a support-note builder.

- **[Brisbane Peaceful Space & Civic AI Summit](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/GAJRA_Earth-Space-AI_Summit/)**. A proposal for a Brisbane gathering around peaceful space, civic AI, care, community resilience, public evidence and creative participation. Its public site introduces thematic rooms, support roles, reference material and a local Markdown support-note builder.

- **[Extreme Matter Atlas](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/extreme-matter-atlas/)**. An interactive matter atlas covering elements, record-holding atoms, crystal predictions, lattices, Chladni patterns, metamaterials and engineering design briefs. Its pages connect material properties and experiments with questions at the edges of current work.

- **[Future of Life 2045](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Future-of-Life-2045/)**. An interactive future vision connecting Luke’s long-running plan with relationships, living systems, intelligence, movement, science and culture. It includes two days in 2045, family-constellation models, Subterranean Cities of Light and a 2022 to 2045 timeline.

## How this could move forward

Advocacy and work with other governments

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could convene appropriate institutional and regional conversations, seek a council-endorsed position where council participation is proposed, and refer national matters through the responsible government channels.

## Who else makes decisions

Other communities, institutions and governments decide their participation. Treaty, immigration, defence, national trade and other national-policy decisions remain with the responsible governments.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Responsible departments, neighbouring councils, MPs, research institutions, community organisations and businesses.

## How related interests would be handled

Relevant interests include paid research, consulting, travel support, an intellectual-property agreement or a partner role connected to an exchange that council is considering.
