# 33 Small wet-sand TBM services for SMBI

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Infrastructure, energy and coasts

## Purpose

The proposal investigates less disruptive and potentially useful ways to add services without joining the Southern Moreton Bay Islands.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Assessment of smaller tunnelling systems would consider particular sewerage or service alignments on individual islands, alongside trenchless alternatives, conventional works and decentralised services.

## Decision route

Council infrastructure and external approvals

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could seek council water, wastewater and infrastructure input on specific island service needs and alignments, and convene the responsible service owners and tunnelling partners.

## Other decision-makers

Each utility and landholder retains its asset and access decisions. Relevant environmental and marine authorities retain their approval roles for the chosen works.

## Possible partners

Council water/wastewater teams and other service owners, island communities, tunnelling researchers and relevant regulators.

## Interests and independent decisions

A council utility study, TBM purchase, service-design licence or works contract connected to Luke’s designs or a future co-operative needs its own independent interest and selection process.
