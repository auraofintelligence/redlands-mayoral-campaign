# 30 Temporary and modular housing options

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Housing and transport

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

The proposal explores safe additional housing while permanent homes are being built and services are being added.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A review of zoning and existing rules would compare secondary dwellings, approved temporary homes and modular or kit homes with the services they need. It would identify possible sites, water and sewerage needs, costs and the steps for trials or proposed rule changes.

## How this could move forward

Council infrastructure and decisions by other authorities

## What the mayor and council could do

Council has planning, local-law, building/plumbing and local infrastructure roles, exercised through lawful decisions and delegations.

## Who else makes decisions

State frameworks, certifiers, utilities, landowners and tenancy requirements also apply. Existing Redland temporary-home rules are tied to works on a permanent residence; wider interim use needs the correct legal pathway.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Council planners, Queensland planning/housing authorities, certifiers, utilities, landowners, developers and housing providers.

## How related interests would be handled

A particular planning, land-access, housing-supply or servicing decision needs independent interest assessment if Luke, Strange But True or an organisation he joins has an interest in the site, design or delivery arrangement.
