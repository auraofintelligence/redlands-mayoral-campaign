# 43 Healthy ageing, HBOT and longevity research

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Health, care and inclusion

## Purpose

Bring healthy-ageing, reablement and longevity questions into a Redlands network of residents, care providers and researchers.

## Possible Redlands action

Develop a shared research agenda around daily function, cognition, quality of life and longevity. Examine HBOT and other therapies on their own terms and in proposed combinations with personal Aura, meaningful activity and participant context.

## Health and research connection

HBOT has established clinical uses, including selected wounds and radiation-related injury. Increased oxygen dissolved in plasma provides a physiological basis for investigating further applications. The wider Genesis and longevity research questions include how HBOT, personal Aura, reflection, meaningful activity, expectation and other therapies contribute separately or together. Compare outcomes over time and across appropriate study groups, retaining the distinction between established indications, mechanisms and outcomes being investigated.

## Decision route

Healthy ageing, HBOT and longevity research

## Mayoral and council lever

Connect this work with council age-friendly planning, convene willing care and research partners, and seek state, federal, institutional and business support for the shared public purposes.

## Other decision-makers

Clinical services and studies sit with the responsible providers and research institutions. Individual participation and care choices remain personal.

## Partners

Older residents and carers, Elders’ organisations, care providers, clinicians, universities, research institutions and government funding partners.

## Specific interest or transaction boundary

Your HBOT equipment, research role or provider interest is relevant if council funds a study, supports access or selects a study partner involving you.
