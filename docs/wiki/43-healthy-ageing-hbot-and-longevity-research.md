# 43 Healthy ageing, HBOT and longevity research

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Health, care and inclusion

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Residents, care providers and researchers could work together on healthy ageing, regaining everyday abilities and questions about living well for longer.

## Words and project names

- **Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura**: Luke’s design for personal artificial intelligence (AI), working with a person’s chosen records to support learning, reflection and everyday tasks. The person chooses the purposes, permissions and what to share.
- **Genesis**: Luke’s proposed series of sixty sessions for personal reflection, health, learning and developing a personal Aura. The participant’s own records and choices connect the process with everyday life.
- **Geode**: The proposed physical equipment and experience settings for Genesis, personal Aura and learning. The source guide describes several HBOT and Geode hardware paths.
- **Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT)**: Breathing oxygen in a pressurised chamber. Clinical services and proposed research have their own providers, participation choices and responsibilities.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Residents and researchers could shape questions about everyday abilities, thinking and memory, quality of life and longevity. HBOT and other therapies would be studied individually and in proposed combinations with personal Aura, meaningful activity and each participant’s circumstances.

## Health and research questions

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) involves breathing oxygen in a pressurised chamber. It has established clinical uses, including selected wounds and radiation-related injury. More oxygen dissolving in blood plasma provides a reason to investigate further uses. The wider Genesis and longevity research would examine HBOT, personal Aura, reflection, meaningful activity, expectations and other therapies, individually and together. Studies would compare outcomes over time and between suitable groups, keeping established treatments separate from questions still being investigated.

## How this could move forward

Health and research partnerships, with relevant council support

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could connect this work with council age-friendly planning, convene willing care and research partners, and seek state, federal, institutional and business support for the shared public purposes.

## Who else makes decisions

Clinical services and studies sit with the responsible providers and research institutions. Individual participation and care choices remain personal.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Older residents and carers, Elders’ organisations, care providers, clinicians, universities, research institutions and government funding partners.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's HBOT equipment, research role or provider interest would be relevant to any council funding of a study, support for access or selection of a study partner involving him.
