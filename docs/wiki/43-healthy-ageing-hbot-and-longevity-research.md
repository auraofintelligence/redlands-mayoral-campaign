# 43 Healthy ageing, HBOT and longevity research

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Health, care and inclusion

## Purpose

The aim is a Redlands network of residents, care providers and researchers working on healthy ageing, reablement and longevity questions.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A shared research agenda would address daily function, cognition, quality of life and longevity. HBOT and other therapies would be examined on their own terms and in proposed combinations with personal Aura, meaningful activity and participant context.

## Health and research connection

HBOT has established clinical uses, including selected wounds and radiation-related injury. Increased oxygen dissolved in plasma provides a physiological basis for investigating further applications. The wider Genesis and longevity research questions include how HBOT, personal Aura, reflection, meaningful activity, expectation and other therapies contribute separately or together. Research would compare outcomes over time and across appropriate study groups, retaining the distinction between established indications, mechanisms and outcomes being investigated.

## Decision route

Healthy ageing, HBOT and longevity research

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could connect this work with council age-friendly planning, convene willing care and research partners, and seek state, federal, institutional and business support for the shared public purposes.

## Other decision-makers

Clinical services and studies sit with the responsible providers and research institutions. Individual participation and care choices remain personal.

## Possible partners

Older residents and carers, Elders’ organisations, care providers, clinicians, universities, research institutions and government funding partners.

## Interests and independent decisions

Luke's HBOT equipment, research role or provider interest would be relevant to any council funding of a study, support for access or selection of a study partner involving him.
