# 36 Tourism, domestic travel and local access

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Local economy and environment

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Residents and visitors could find local activities and plan travel more easily, with clearer transport, access and cost information.

## Words and project names

- **Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura**: Luke’s design for personal artificial intelligence (AI), working with a person’s chosen records to support learning, reflection and everyday tasks. The person chooses the purposes, permissions and what to share.
- **Genesis**: Luke’s proposed series of sixty sessions for personal reflection, health, learning and developing a personal Aura. The participant’s own records and choices connect the process with everyday life.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Clear transport and accessibility information could connect residents and visitors with local suppliers, culture, nature, music and learning. Participants could choose how personal Aura supports their interests and travel records. Local tourism operators could also access practical AI learning.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Aura Tourism](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-tourism/)**. A personal-intelligence tourism proposal connecting music, sport, culture, nature and reflection with development of the qualified self. It starts on Minjerribah and explores travel and learning through Redlands, Brisbane, Australia and the 2032 horizon.

- **[Australian World Travel](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Australian-world-travel/)**. A travel-planning guide for an Australian aiming to visit every country. It combines route planning, country research, diplomatic contacts, document preparation, itinerary tools and a browser-local logistics tracker.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could convene local tourism, cultural, accessibility and learning partners around visitor resources and experiences, and seek council support through its tourism, public-place and community roles.

## Who else makes decisions

Operators and cultural hosts choose the experiences they offer; transport providers and the responsible public bodies retain decisions about their services and infrastructure.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Local tourism operators, transport providers, accessibility users, cultural custodians and Queensland tourism/education programs.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's tourism tools, content or delivery role would be relevant to any council purchase of visitor resources, funding of an experience or provider selection involving him.
