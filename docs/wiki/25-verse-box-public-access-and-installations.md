# 25 Verse Box public access and installations

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Public spaces, arts and local communication

## Purpose

Verse Box stations and supported sessions would provide accessible public access for useful personal, creative and community work.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Verse Box stations could be installed and operated with willing libraries and community hosts, with agreed purposes, equipment, accessibility, support, maintenance and session arrangements. Demonstrations would use sample records; personal sessions would provide private handover, export and deletion.

## Decision route

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could convene suitable hosts and support council consideration of stations, public access and assistance within its venues or programmes.

## Other decision-makers

Hosts and providers agree the station's operation and responsibilities. Each participant chooses their personal records and any further Aura or Genesis pathway.

## Possible partners

Libraries, community venues, learning/accessibility partners, equipment providers and willing participants.

## Interests and independent decisions

Luke's station design, software or Strange But True would be relevant to an interest assessment if council purchased, licensed, hosted or funded a Verse Box installation.
