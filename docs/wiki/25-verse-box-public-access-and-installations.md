# 25 Verse Box public access and installations

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Public spaces, arts and local communication

## Purpose

Provide accessible public stations and supported sessions for useful personal, creative and community work.

## Possible Redlands action

Install and operate Verse Box stations with willing libraries and community hosts. Agree the station's purposes, equipment, accessibility, support, maintenance and session arrangements. Use sample records for demonstrations and provide private handover, export and deletion for personal sessions.

## Decision route

Community partnership

## Mayoral and council lever

Convene suitable hosts and support council consideration of stations, public access and assistance within its venues or programmes.

## Other decision-makers

Hosts and providers agree the station's operation and responsibilities. Each participant chooses their personal records and any further Aura or Genesis pathway.

## Partners

Libraries, community venues, learning/accessibility partners, equipment providers and willing participants.

## Specific interest or transaction boundary

Your station design, software or Strange But True is relevant if council purchases, licences, hosts or funds a Verse Box installation.
