# 25 Verse Box public access and installations

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Public spaces, arts and local communication

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Verse Box is designed as a supported public starting point for creating an Aura of Intelligence: a personal digital twin developed through self-reflection and patterning. The aim is to extend a person’s intelligence so they can learn more, learn faster and act in the world with greater intent towards Joyful Responsible Abundance.

## Words and project names

- **Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura**: Luke’s design for personal artificial intelligence (AI), working with a person’s chosen records to support learning, reflection and everyday tasks. The person chooses the purposes, permissions and what to share.
- **Genesis**: Luke’s proposed series of sixty sessions for personal reflection, health, learning and developing a personal Aura. The participant’s own records and choices connect the process with everyday life.
- **Verse Box**: A proposed portable public station where people could try personal Aura, learning and community activities with support from a host. The trial guide covers equipment and clear handling of personal records.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Willing libraries and community hosts could provide Verse Box stations with suitable equipment, access, support and maintenance. A personal session could help someone reflect on their chosen experiences, knowledge and intentions, begin developing their digital twin, and explore how its patterning supports learning and purposeful action. Demonstrations would use sample records. Personal sessions would include a private handover and clear ways to take away or delete personal information.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Verse Box](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-verse-box/)**. A portable public introduction to personal Aura of Intelligence, learning, Community Hours and community participation. The trial guide covers the visitor experience, equipment, hosts, access and personal-session handover.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could convene suitable hosts and support council consideration of stations, public access and assistance within its venues or programs.

## Who else makes decisions

Hosts and providers agree the station's operation and responsibilities. Each participant chooses their personal records and any further Aura or Genesis pathway.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Libraries, community venues, learning/accessibility partners, equipment providers and willing participants.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's station design, software or Strange But True would need independent consideration if council purchased, licensed, hosted or funded a Verse Box installation.
