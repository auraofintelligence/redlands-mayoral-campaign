# 17 Children and youth learning and connection

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Health, care and inclusion

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Young people could have more opportunities to learn, make, contribute and build trusted relationships with people of different generations.

## Words and project names

- **Artificial intelligence (AI)**: Computer systems that can generate or analyse material such as text, images and data. Their output needs checking against the purpose and the source.
- **Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura**: Luke’s design for personal artificial intelligence (AI), working with a person’s chosen records to support learning, reflection and everyday tasks. The person chooses the purposes, permissions and what to share.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Supervised learning and creative activities could use AI tools suited to participants’ ages and connect with existing youth services. Hosts would follow the relevant permissions, child-safety arrangements and school or care-authority requirements. Young people’s agreement would be sought in ways suited to their age and ability to make the decision.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Aura Children and Young People](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-child-services/)**. A guide to Aura Genesis for children and young people in Australian care. It connects chosen-interest creative learning, self-understanding, law, family and cultural identity, mentors, contribution and opportunities for adulthood.

- **[Aura Teacher Training](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-teacher-training/)**. A sixty-day adult learning progression connecting personal Aura development, self-understanding, critical and ethical AI use, and teaching. Daily readings and studio guides include practical making, teach-back, evidence and learning portfolios.

- **[Verse Box](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-verse-box/)**. A portable public introduction to personal Aura of Intelligence, learning, Community Hours and community participation. The trial guide covers the visitor experience, equipment, hosts, access and personal-session handover.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

Council could support spaces, opportunities, partnerships and community programs for young people.

## Who else makes decisions

Education, children in care, clinical services, police and youth justice remain with the responsible authorities. Their lawful consent and safeguarding arrangements would apply.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Young people, schools, youth/care services, lawful care authorities, mentors and existing community organisations.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke’s youth-learning materials, software or delivery role would be a relevant interest if council funded or commissioned a program involving them.
