# 46 Care, learning and research site partnerships

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Health, care and inclusion

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Willing hosts across Redlands could provide places for care, learning, personal intelligence and longevity work, with clear agreements for each location.

## Words and project names

- **Geode**: The proposed physical equipment and experience settings for Genesis, personal Aura and learning. The source guide describes several HBOT and Geode hardware paths.
- **GAJRA Earth**: Luke’s proposed network connecting contribution, learning, events, travel, research and shared capability. A Redlands organisation would have its own members and agreed rules.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Conversations with willing hosts would include the 82 Claytons Road invitation and other suitable mainland and island locations. Each agreement would explain the services offered, who runs them, access, ownership and ongoing responsibilities.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[82 Claytons Road: A Gift for Generations](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Anglican-Diocese-82-Claytons-Amity/)**. A land-gift and partnership invitation for 82 Claytons Road, Amity, prepared for Anglican Church Southern Queensland. The proposal brings together dementia support, aged care, longevity research, learning and community participation, with its manuscript and document library.

- **[Minjerribah Site and Service Ideas](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/multi-site-Minjerribah-network/)**. A source-linked workbench for 7 Mile and other Minjerribah site and service ideas. Its separate place and service pages cover living, residencies, housing, recovery, care, longevity, making, training and organisational arrangements.

- **[Aura for Adaptive Whole of Life Support](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-whole-of-life/)**. An Aura of Intelligence support proposal for adults living with dementia or disability, people beginning aged care, and their supporters and providers. Its reading site covers personal identity, memory, choices, relationships, support and connected research pathways.

## How this could move forward

Agreements with willing property owners, hosts and providers

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could bring landowners, care providers, community organisations, businesses and funding bodies together. Council could consider its relevant planning, community, access and partnership roles.

## Who else makes decisions

Property owners decide what they offer. Hosts and providers decide their services, operations and agreements. Each location has its own decision route.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Anglican Church Southern Queensland and Anglicare as possible conversation partners, other willing landowners, care providers, Elders’ organisations, community hosts, researchers and educators.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's property, lease, host role or organisation interest would be relevant to any council site funding, provision of premises or hosting agreement involving him.
