# 34 Spoil reuse, erosion protection and reefs

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Infrastructure, energy and coasts

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Sand and other material excavated during works could have further uses in suitable coastal protection and artificial reef projects.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Each batch of excavated material and each proposed receiving site would be assessed for suitable reuse. Possible coastal protection and engineered artificial reef uses would be compared with approaches that work with natural coastal processes and other available options.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Sandworm Subterranean Systems](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/sandworm-subterranean-systems/)**. A subterranean-systems research workbench exploring autonomous transport arteries and continuous town connections on Minjerribah. Its questions include tunnelling, erosion, spoil reuse, geopolymer materials, artificial reefs, wave energy and sand heat storage.

- **[Straddie Clean Energy Superpower](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-clean-energy-superpower/)**. A clean-energy research atlas for Minjerribah, covering solar and batteries, solar thermal, heat storage, hydrogen, desalination, power sharing, pumped hydro, wave and tidal energy, and wind. Its topic pages and Markdown builders help develop source-linked questions and energy scenarios.

## How this could move forward

Council infrastructure and decisions by other authorities

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could convene council coastal and environment teams, custodians and research partners around material testing and identified coastal or reef applications within the relevant council responsibilities.

## Who else makes decisions

Material owners, receiving-site managers and relevant coastal, marine and environmental authorities decide the applicable material and site requirements.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Council coastal/environment teams, material-testing specialists, marine/coastal researchers, custodians and regulators.

## How related interests would be handled

Any council material-transfer, testing, coastal-work or reef contract would need independent assessment where Luke had an interest in the excavated material, design or receiving project.
