# 37 Wildlife, pollinators and local stewardship

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Local economy and environment

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Local bee-resilience work, wildlife care and ecological learning could receive support through the people and organisations responsible for each activity.

## What this could mean for Redlands

The bee-resilience proposal brings local beekeepers, people working with bees and allies together around hive observations, research, learning and the proposed lab. Wildlife and habitat work would continue through its own organisations and separately agreed activities.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Bees & Allies](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/bees-and-allies/)**. A beekeeper-focused proposal for a locally led Straddie bee-resilience lab. It covers hive observations and census, biosecurity, surveillance and research, a digital-twin-first island-lab model, and a possible institute and field-school pathway.

- **[Wildlife Rescue Minjerribah](https://wildlife-rescue-minjerribah.auraofintelligence.workers.dev/)**. An offline-first smartphone app for wildlife reporting and volunteer response on Minjerribah. It combines an island map, incident location, draft animal-specific triage, locally saved case records and responder-alert handoffs.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could bring council environment teams into the bee-resilience conversation with local participants and invited university or agency partners. Council could separately consider proposed support for wildlife and habitat initiatives within its land and program responsibilities.

## Who else makes decisions

Landholders, wildlife carers, custodians and responsible environmental authorities retain their permissions and specialist functions.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Local beekeepers, bee people and allies; invited universities and agencies as supporting partners. Wildlife and habitat initiatives have their own licensed carers, custodians, land managers and community participants.

## How related interests would be handled

The proposal calls for transparent land-access, grant and service criteria, with independent handling of any specific council benefit to a project or organisation associated with Luke.
