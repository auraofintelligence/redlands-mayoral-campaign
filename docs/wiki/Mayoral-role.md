## What the office lets you do

<p>The mayor chairs council, provides strategic direction to the CEO within the Act, appraises the CEO, participates in standing committees and speaks for council. Council makes collective policy and budget decisions; the CEO manages administration and staff. Mayoral directions cannot override a council resolution or adopted document, direct employee appointment or discipline, or require an unlawful act. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a01">Current Act, sections 12, 13, 104 and 170</a>; <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a02">Redland roles</a>.</p>

## Advancing your ideas without mixing the roles

<p>Authorship of an idea does not by itself decide every conflict question. The assessment is about the actual matter, your interests and whether you may participate under the current law. Financial and non-financial interests both need attention. Disclosure alone does not authorise continued participation. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a01">Current Act</a>; <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a07">interests guidance</a>.</p><p>Frame the public outcome first: lower household costs, useful skills, resilience or better access. Council can then consider transparent criteria, available approaches and delivery arrangements. If a particular decision could benefit you, Strange But True, your IP or equipment, or an organisation you later establish or join, obtain independent advice and apply the required process before taking part in that matter.</p><p>Keep personal and Strange But True finances, accounts and information distinct from council. General business conversations and public capability support should be separated from a live tender: Redland’s tender instructions direct tenderers away from councillor contact about the tender. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a06">Tender guidance</a>. Any licence, donation, loan or access to your equipment needs clear terms and independent handling where interests arise.</p><p>For discussions with other governments and businesses, be clear whether you speak personally, for a future organisation if one is formed, or for council under an authorised position.</p>
