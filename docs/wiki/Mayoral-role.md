# Mayoral role and responsibilities

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names)

The mayor, councillors, council administration and other governments have different jobs. Understanding those roles helps explain how a local idea can move towards a decision.

## What the mayor and council can do

<p>The mayor chairs council meetings, speaks for council and has specific responsibilities for providing direction to the chief executive officer (CEO). The elected council votes on policy and budgets. The CEO manages day-to-day services and staff. Mayoral directions must stay within the Act and council’s decisions, including limits relating to staff appointments, discipline and unlawful conduct. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a01">Local Government Act, sections 12, 13, 104 and 170</a>; <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a02">Redland roles and responsibilities</a>.</p>

## Public benefit and independent decisions

<p>Writing an idea does not by itself settle a conflict-of-interest question. The actual decision and the officeholder’s financial and non-financial interests matter. Declaring an interest does not automatically allow someone to keep taking part in that decision. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a01">Local Government Act</a>; <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a07">registers of interests guidance</a>.</p>
<p>The proposals begin with public purposes: lower avoidable costs, useful skills, reliable services, community connection and fair access. Council could compare ways to meet those purposes using clear criteria. A decision that could benefit Luke, Strange But True, his work or equipment, or an organisation he later establishes or joins would need the applicable interest process and independent advice before his participation.</p>
<p>Personal and Strange But True accounts and information would remain separate from council. General business support and a live tender have different rules. Council’s tender guidance explains the limits on contact with councillors. Any licence, donation, loan or use of Luke’s equipment would need clear terms and independent handling where interests arise. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a06">Council tender guidance</a>.</p>
<p>Discussions would make clear whether Luke is speaking personally, for an organisation or on behalf of an agreed council position.</p>
