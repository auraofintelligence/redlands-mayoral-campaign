# Mayoral role and responsibilities

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home)

The mayor, councillors, council administration and other governments have different responsibilities. These distinctions explain how the proposals could be advanced. The official source checks below were recorded on 8 November 2026.

## What the mayor and council can do

<p>The mayor chairs council, provides strategic direction to the CEO within the Act, appraises the CEO, participates in standing committees and speaks for council. Council makes collective policy and budget decisions; the CEO manages administration and staff. Mayoral directions cannot override a council resolution or adopted document, direct employee appointment or discipline, or require an unlawful act. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a01">Current Act, sections 12, 13, 104 and 170</a>; <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a02">Redland roles</a>.</p>

## Public benefit and independent decisions

<p>Authorship of an idea does not by itself settle every conflict question. The assessment depends on the actual matter, the officeholder’s financial and non-financial interests, and the current law. Disclosure alone does not authorise continued participation. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a01">Current Act</a>; <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a07">interests guidance</a>.</p>
<p>The proposals start with public outcomes such as lower household costs, useful skills, resilience and better access. Council can consider transparent criteria, available approaches and delivery arrangements. A specific decision that could benefit Luke, Strange But True, his intellectual property or equipment, or an organisation he later establishes or joins would require the applicable interest process and independent advice before participation.</p>
<p>Personal and Strange But True finances, accounts and information would remain separate from council. General business discussions and capability support are distinct from a live tender: Redland’s tender instructions direct tenderers away from councillor contact about the tender. <a href="https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Official-sources#a06">Tender guidance</a>. Any licence, donation, loan or access to Luke’s equipment would need clear terms and independent handling where interests arise.</p>
<p>Discussions with governments and businesses would distinguish Luke’s personal views, the position of any future organisation and an authorised council position.</p>
