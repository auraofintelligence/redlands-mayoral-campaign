# 28 Disaster-ready kiosks and resilient local information

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Disaster readiness and recovery

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Disaster-ready kiosks aim to help people find reliable local information, including during power or internet outages.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Site assessment and a trial would cover information that remains available without the internet, backup power and communications, and accessible preparedness material. Official warnings and updates would come through the authorised agencies and publishing arrangements.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Straddie Disaster Kiosks](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-disaster-kiosks/)**. A proposal for everyday island screens that can also support information access during disruption. It covers rugged noticeboards, venue publishing, camera questions, media tools and the planning requirements for resilient kiosk operation.

- **[Aura Disaster Resilience](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-disaster-resilience/)**. A community-capability and disaster-resilience design program covering all-weather kiosks, local information, personal Aura, power, communications, preparedness and continuing recovery. Its wiki and source library organise the questions, design options, evidence and decisions.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

Council's disaster responsibilities and the Local Disaster Management Group provide the route for integrating kiosks with approved information workflows.

## Who else makes decisions

Warning agencies, emergency responders, utilities and the other members of the Local Disaster Management Group retain their operational responsibilities.

## People and organisations who could be involved

The Local Disaster Management Group, council’s disaster team, authorised warning agencies, utilities, venue hosts and community and accessibility representatives.

## How related interests would be handled

Kiosk sites, equipment and support contracts would need independent assessment where Luke's designs or equipment could benefit. Responsibilities for publishing authorised warnings would remain clear.
