# 20 500 Queens regional leadership and human mentoring

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Learning, work and leadership

## Purpose

500 Queens aims to create opportunities for emerging female leaders to practise long-range civic decision-making and build networks.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Leadership exercises could be adapted to local long-term planning, with human mentors invited through Brisbane, Logan, Toowoomba, Gold Coast, Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast and other interested councils.

## Decision route

Advocacy and intergovernmental partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could invite neighbouring councils to a shared leadership and mentoring programme, with each council choosing its contribution.

## Other decision-makers

Human mentors and other councils agree their own participation; the mayor cannot appoint them or commit their resources.

## Possible partners

Brisbane, Logan, Toowoomba, Gold Coast, Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast and other willing councils, educators and mentors.

## Interests and independent decisions

Luke's mentoring, programme or partner role would be relevant to an interest assessment if council funded the network, selected its partners or supported an organisation he joined.
