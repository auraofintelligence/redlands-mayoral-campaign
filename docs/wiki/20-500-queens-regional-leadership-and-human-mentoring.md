# 20 500 Queens regional leadership and human mentoring

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Learning, work and leadership

## Purpose

Create opportunities for emerging female leaders to practise long-range civic decision-making and build networks.

## Possible Redlands action

Adapt leadership exercises to local long-term planning and invite real mentors through Brisbane, Logan, Toowoomba, Gold Coast, Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast and other interested councils.

## Decision route

Advocacy and intergovernmental partnership

## Mayoral and council lever

Invite neighbouring councils to a shared leadership and mentoring programme, with each choosing its contribution.

## Other decision-makers

Human mentors and other councils agree their own participation; the office cannot appoint them or commit their resources.

## Partners

Brisbane, Logan, Toowoomba, Gold Coast, Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast and other willing councils, educators and mentors.

## Specific interest or transaction boundary

Your mentoring, programme or partner role is relevant if council funds the network, selects its partners or supports an organisation you join.
