# 20 500 Queens regional leadership and human mentoring

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Learning, work and leadership

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

500 Queens is a women’s enterprise and leadership proposal. It aims to give emerging female leaders opportunities to practise decisions that affect the coming decades and learn with experienced human mentors.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Leadership exercises could focus on local decisions with effects well into the future. Human mentors could be invited through Brisbane, Logan, Toowoomba, Gold Coast, Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast and other interested councils.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[500 Queens Venture Capital](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/500-Queens-VC-2026/)**. A women’s enterprise and leadership proposal for developing CEOs and decision-makers. It brings together AI Queen profiles, enterprise guides, startup suggestions, source readers and private planning tools.

- **[Queens Venture](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Queens_Venture/)**. A regenerative venture architecture called the Queens System, connecting women-led stewardship, environmental regeneration and civilisation-scale themes. Its interactive report develops proposed roles, venture themes, an investment thesis and a staged capital pathway.

## How this could move forward

Advocacy and work with other governments

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could invite neighbouring councils to a shared leadership and mentoring program, with each council choosing its contribution.

## Who else makes decisions

Human mentors and other councils agree their own participation; the mayor cannot appoint them or commit their resources.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Brisbane, Logan, Toowoomba, Gold Coast, Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast and other willing councils, educators and mentors.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's mentoring, program or partner role would need independent consideration if council funded the network, selected its partners or supported an organisation he joined.
