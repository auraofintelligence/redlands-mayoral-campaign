# 07 Open data and public digital twin sandbox

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · AI capability and accountable council

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Residents and businesses could use public information and digital models of places to compare ideas, test assumptions and submit proposals others can examine.

## Words and project names

- **Artificial intelligence (AI)**: Computer systems that can generate or analyse material such as text, images and data. Their output needs checking against the purpose and the source.
- **Digital twin**: A digital model of a real place, person or system. The purpose, information and permissions vary with what is being modelled.
- **Data sovereignty**: Who has authority over data, including where it is kept, what it may be used for and who can access it. Cultural knowledge has its own custodians and permissions.

## A practical example

Like a model used before a building project, a digital twin lets people compare options before work starts. The usefulness of the result depends on its information and assumptions.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Council could list public datasets, explain the rights attached to them and identify suitable uses. Digital twins are models of real places or systems. Selected models could let people compare proposals using public information, made-up test data or information with identifying details appropriately removed. Assumptions, updates and ways to share results would be recorded with the relevant data owners.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Scan to Twin](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-digital-twin-explainer/)**. A plain-English guide to developing community-owned digital twins of Minjerribah from phone scans. It explains capture-to-drawing, island modelling, nested twins and the surrounding governance and shared-computing questions.

- **[The Straddie Twins](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-twins/)**. A public companion guide to two separate digital twins of Minjerribah. It explains their systems, layers, boards, packs and instruments, distinguishes different kinds of modelled information, and provides routes into each build.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could seek a council data-access policy and useful public simulation services. Staff and data owners would determine approved datasets, update responsibilities and integration.

## Who else makes decisions

Data owners, privacy law, licences, cultural authority, security and rights over third-party information constrain what can be released.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Council information/privacy teams, planners, utilities, businesses, researchers and relevant Traditional Owner data custodians.

## How related interests would be handled

Any council decision involving a data licence, simulation tool, software licence or data-service agreement would identify relevant interests, including Luke’s where applicable.
