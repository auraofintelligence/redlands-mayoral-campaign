# 21 Olympics community capability and lasting benefit

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Learning, work and leadership

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Brisbane 2032 could create opportunities for Redlands residents and organisations, with skills, connections and local services that remain useful after the Games.

## Words and project names

- **Community Hours (C-hours)**: A way to recognise verified voluntary human contribution and its outcomes. The source introduction describes one verified hour of contribution with no monetary equivalent. Participation and information sharing are choices.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A Redlands discussion with the responsible Games and state bodies could cover accessible visitor information, volunteering, paid work, local arts and business, and skills and services useful beyond 2032. Each independent project would explain its own connection with the Games.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[C-Hour Introduction](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/)**. An introduction and reference library for Community Hours: one verified hour of voluntary human contribution, with no monetary equivalent. It explores locally chosen recognition, reciprocity, personal intelligence and shared capability.

- **[Sovereign Capacity Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/sovereign-capacity-lab/)**. A browser-based research and scenario laboratory for mining, materials, power, repairable robots and locally governed infrastructure. Its workbenches connect physical resource accounts, distributed compute, manufacturing, Australian industry and a 2032 planning horizon.

## How this could move forward

Advocacy and work with other governments

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could represent a council-endorsed Redlands position in regional, state and Games discussions, connect local organisations with the responsible program owners, and seek clear local-benefit and access arrangements.

## Who else makes decisions

Games and state program owners determine their activities, recruitment, branding, purchasing and funding. Local partners choose their contributions through the relevant arrangements.

## People and organisations who could be involved

State/Games program owners, neighbouring councils, volunteer organisations, tourism businesses and arts/community hosts.

## How related interests would be handled

Any personal or business interest Luke has in a Games-related sponsorship, paid role, supplier arrangement or council-supported activity would need to be identified.
