# 52 Cultural intelligence nodes and locally governed federation

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Redlands sovereign capacity and civic establishments

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Cultural intelligence nodes are proposed locally run services for community knowledge, computing and learning. They could share selected resources with other places by agreement, while keeping local authority.

## Words and project names

- **Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura**: Luke’s approach to personal AI, built around a person’s chosen records, purposes, permissions and relationship with the system.
- **Community Hours (C-hours)**: A way to recognise verified voluntary human contribution and its outcomes. The source introduction describes one verified hour of contribution with no monetary equivalent. Participation and information sharing are choices.
- **Sovereign capacity / sovereign compute**: The practical ability to provide and govern useful computing, skills, equipment and services, including decisions about where data is kept and how systems are run.
- **Data sovereignty**: Who has authority over data, including where it is kept, what it may be used for and who can access it. Cultural knowledge has its own custodians and permissions.
- **A node and a federation**: A node is a local service within a wider network. A federation is an arrangement for those local services to work together while keeping agreed local authority.

## A practical example

Neighbouring libraries can share selected resources while each remains responsible for its own collection. Cultural intelligence nodes could cooperate in a similar way, under agreed permissions.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A cultural intelligence node is a locally run service for computing, tools and knowledge. Its agreements would cover personal and community memory, cultural custody, permissions, AI models, paid technical work, teaching and maintenance. It could use the computing services in entry 39 and a host place in entry 49. Nodes could exchange selected resources while participants keep control of their access keys, identities and decisions.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Ready SET Co-op Cultural Intelligence Node](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ready-set-co-op-cultural-intelligence-node/)**. A proposal for one cultural intelligence compute node in Dunwich, using a GB200 NVL72-class rack to explore workloads, power, cooling, skills and place. It develops cultural custody and permissions, local project uses, co-operative services, place modelling and selected node-to-node exchange.

- **[Aura Civic Establishment](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-civic-establishment/)**. A shared civic-place proposal exploring a round-the-clock mix of activities, rooms, public access, KYC, computing and operating arrangements. It includes overlap tools, capacity calculators and a Brisbane candidate-site study.

- **[Installing the Missing Middle](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/installing-the-missing-middle/)**. A national installation-business plan for a federation of locally owned community-compute nodes. It combines computing, useful heat, backup power, installation learning, shared ownership, finance and hardware renewal.

- **[Sovereign Capacity Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/sovereign-capacity-lab/)**. A browser-based research and scenario laboratory for mining, materials, power, repairable robots and locally governed infrastructure. Its workbenches connect physical resource accounts, distributed compute, manufacturing, Australian industry and a 2032 planning horizon.

- **[Ready SET Co-op Trust Hub](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ready-set-co-op-trust-hub/)**. A trust-building, co-working and asset-sharing pathway toward Ready S.E.T. Co-op on Minjerribah. It explores local support work, training and paid roles, grant preparation and a possible community base at 9 Ballow Road.

- **[Aura Matrix Studio](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-matrix-studio/)**. A personal interface for everyday context such as life records, relationships, reminders, learning, skills and travel, organised through a 12 by 24 matrix. It collects a minimum viable self as a foundation for later personal-model development.

## What it needs to work

Match equipment, storage, electricity, cooling, networks, security, skilled operators and backup arrangements to the work being done. Personal Aura, cultural knowledge, creative work, public models, robotics and research each need suitable permissions and resources. Local authority and voluntary sharing between nodes are part of the design. Paid technical work and C-hour recognition would have separate records.

## How this could move forward

Council, regional, state, federal and business cooperation

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could bring willing hosts, cultural custodians, technical operators, educators and business partners together, and seek council support for public functions alongside state, federal or regional investment in infrastructure and operating capability.

## Who else makes decisions

Technical operation and cultural permission are distinct responsibilities. Each participating person, organisation and place decides what may be viewed, stored, transcribed, modelled, shared or published within the law. Infrastructure owners, service operators and funding bodies make their own agreements.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Local communities and cultural custodians, Quandamooka organisations where they choose to participate, compute/network/energy providers, paid technical operators, educators, co-operative participants, researchers, businesses and neighbouring councils.

## How related interests would be handled

Relevant interests include an interest in a node operator, hosting agreement, licensed technology, paid technical role or data-service contract proposed for council support.
