# 44 Redlands sovereign capacity for the age of superintelligence

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Redlands sovereign capacity and civic establishments

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

[Begin with Joyful Responsible Abundance](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/joyful-responsible-abundance/index.html) for the whole-system direction behind this Redlands proposal.

## The idea

Joyful Responsible Abundance provides the starting framework for Redlands sovereign capacity: useful intelligence, skills and productive resources that people can help govern and benefit from.

## Words and project names

- **Artificial intelligence (AI)**: Computer systems that can generate or analyse material such as text, images and data. Their output needs checking against the purpose and the source.
- **Sovereign capacity / sovereign compute**: The practical ability to provide and govern useful computing, skills, equipment and services, including decisions about where data is kept and how systems are run.
- **The missing middle**: The people, places, equipment and ongoing work needed between an idea and a service people can actually use. The linked project develops a plan for locally owned community computing.

## What this could mean for Redlands

The Redlands sovereign-capacity agenda would be developed with councillors, neighbouring councils, state and federal government, businesses, educators and community organisations. It would define shared public purposes, the connections between independent projects, each participant's responsibilities, and the agreements or investments sought.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Sovereign Capacity Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/sovereign-capacity-lab/)**. A browser-based research and scenario laboratory for mining, materials, power, repairable robots and locally governed infrastructure. Its workbenches connect physical resource accounts, distributed compute, manufacturing, Australian industry and a 2032 planning horizon.

- **[Aura Civic Establishment](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-civic-establishment/)**. A shared civic-place proposal exploring a round-the-clock mix of activities, rooms, public access, KYC, computing and operating arrangements. It includes overlap tools, capacity calculators and a Brisbane candidate-site study.

- **[Installing the Missing Middle](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/installing-the-missing-middle/)**. A national installation-business plan for a federation of locally owned community-compute nodes. It combines computing, useful heat, backup power, installation learning, shared ownership, finance and hardware renewal.

- **[Ready SET Co-op Cultural Intelligence Node](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ready-set-co-op-cultural-intelligence-node/)**. A proposal for one cultural intelligence compute node in Dunwich, using a GB200 NVL72-class rack to explore workloads, power, cooling, skills and place. It develops cultural custody and permissions, local project uses, co-operative services, place modelling and selected node-to-node exchange.

- **[Joyful Responsible Abundance](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/joyful-responsible-abundance/)**. An educational website introducing Luke's whole-system proposal and his paper on superintelligence and Australian sovereignty. Reading paths, source documents and calculators connect people, productive capacity, civic institutions and wider world questions.

## Why this matters

Joyful Responsible Abundance connects personal agency, health, learning, useful work, public evidence and shared computing with the capacity to shape decisions. The Redlands agenda applies that whole-system direction locally, responding to the proposed 2027/2028 superintelligence horizon with urgency to build capability. The Sovereign Capacity Lab and the 7 November manuscript connect intelligence to industrial buildout and Australian participation. The proposed response is practical capability, local authority and diverse partnerships that reduce dependence on US- or China-centred systems. The different projects remain independently governed.

## What it needs to work

Useful work people need provides the starting point, followed by the equipment, people, energy and materials required to deliver it. Physical production, working equipment, available infrastructure, finance, ownership and public outcomes would have separate accounts.

## Everyday uses

Shared capacity can support household bill and legal tools, personal Aura and Verse Box, tourism, C-hour activity, the civic workforce, food growing, care, public simulations, repair, infrastructure and disaster resilience.

## How this could move forward

Council, regional, state, federal and business cooperation

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could convene the strategic conversation, build support for relevant council policy and partnerships, and represent an authorised Redlands position with other governments and institutions.

## Who else makes decisions

Council governs its own services, assets, plans and spending. National industrial, energy, mining, research, trade and security policy involves other governments; grids, manufacturers, landowners and independent organisations make their own commitments.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Redland councillors and staff, neighbouring councils, state and federal departments and MPs, universities, manufacturers, energy/service owners, local businesses, community organisations and relevant cultural custodians.

## How related interests would be handled

Relevant interests include a proposed governing role, asset interest or named funding allocation for Luke or an associated organisation within the shared capacity agreements.
