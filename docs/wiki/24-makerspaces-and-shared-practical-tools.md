# 24 Makerspaces and shared practical tools

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Food, repair and community wealth

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Shared workshops, tools and practical learning could make repairs, gardening and making more affordable and accessible.

## Words and project names

- **Community Hours (C-hours)**: A way to recognise verified voluntary human contribution and its outcomes. The source introduction describes one verified hour of contribution with no monetary equivalent. Participation and information sharing are choices.

## A practical example

A shared workshop can give several households access to a tool and the skills to use it, with agreed bookings, upkeep and costs.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A map of existing workshops, sheds and community equipment could show what is available across council divisions. Willing hosts could then develop access, maintenance and practical learning sessions together.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Straddie Maker-Space Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-makerspace-lab/)**. A proposal for a light-industrial maker-space in Dunwich / Goompi. It explores useful making, repair and right-to-repair, shared tools, compact workshop design, recycling, upcycling and local material knowledge.

- **[Infinity Creative Workshop](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/infinity-creative-workshop/)**. A training website for music videos, educational films, shorts, Blender and DaVinci Resolve, with practical exercises, design choices, provider comparisons and cost tools. It also develops Queens mentorship applications for creative and learning work.

- **[Community Club Builder: Sandy Sports](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/community-club-builder-sandy-sports/)**. A practical playbook for forming and running a community club, using a Minjerribah sand-sports club as its worked example. It covers governance, inclusive leadership, membership, shared administration, AI assistance and funding preparation.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could seek council support for shared facilities, equipment access and practical learning through libraries, venues and community partnerships.

## Who else makes decisions

Tool hosts manage their equipment and access arrangements; specialist electrical, trade and other regulated work retains its relevant professional responsibilities.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Libraries, men’s sheds, makerspaces, clubs, trainers and shared-equipment hosts.

## How related interests would be handled

Access and equipment purchases would need transparent arrangements. Council support involving Luke's equipment, designs, business or an organisation he joined would require independent handling.
