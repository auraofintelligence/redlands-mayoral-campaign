# 02 Legal information and council rules navigator

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Household savings and fairness

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

The legal information engine could help residents find the rule that applies, gather the right documents and reach the right service with less running around.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A Redlands version could explain council processes, common consumer issues and routes to help. It would show the source, its date, which laws apply, and the steps for making and following up a complaint.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Australian Legal Engine: Read the engine guide](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-law-2012-lukes-relevance/)**. Offline software for parsing, indexing and searching Australian Acts held on the user’s own machine. It retrieves exact provisions with addresses and source dates, traces legislative cross-references and prepares cited handoffs for further research.

- **[Australian Law: Luke's Relevance](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-law-2012-lukes-relevance/)**. A visual record of Luke’s 2012–2013 legal read-through and the relevance-based method he developed. It contains the source-corpus manifest, legal-engine design and civic pathways for researching and changing rules.

- **[Legal Memory Workbench](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/legal-memory-workbench/)**. A browser workbench for preparing personal or project-specific legal-information context as Markdown. It helps map activities and obligations, identify jurisdictions and sources, and organise evidence and questions for the user’s chosen AI workflow.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could seek council support for a rules and referral navigator covering council processes, with dated sources and connections to community legal assistance.

## Who else makes decisions

The navigator helps people locate rules and routes. Qualified professionals and the relevant complaint, tribunal or court bodies retain their own functions and decisions.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Council legal/customer-service teams, community legal centres, qualified lawyers and relevant complaint bodies.

## How related interests would be handled

Any council licence or contract involving Luke’s legal-engine intellectual property would require independent assessment. Case referrals and document access would use clear consent and impartial criteria.
