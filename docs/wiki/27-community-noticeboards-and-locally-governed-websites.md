# 27 Community noticeboards and locally governed websites

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Public spaces, arts and local communication

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Community noticeboards and locally run websites could make local information easier to find and give communities more control over how it is shared.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A trial could combine accessible public digital noticeboards with a website that works on slower connections. Optional subscriptions could help people follow local activities, services and preparedness information.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Straddie Noticeboard Network](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-noticeboard-network/)**. A public-noticeboard network design for Minjerribah, with publisher categories, sample notices, device manifests and a shared publishing pipeline. Each publisher keeps its own identity while public display information stays separate from private context.

- **[Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative (Ready SET Co-op) Media](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ready-set-co-op-hyperlocal-media/)**. A local-first mixed-media production-network proposal for Minjerribah, connecting stories, practical media skills, tools and employment pathways. It includes contributor route finding, production-system design and examples of human-reviewed publishing.

- **[Quandamooka Country Events Engine](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/quandamooka-country-events-engine/)**. An event atlas and planning toolkit for Quandamooka Country, covering places, suppliers, permissions, transport, run sheets, public notices and aftercare. Its browser builders produce portable Markdown briefs for organisers.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could seek council support for accessible public information channels and community publishing partnerships, with clear editorial and moderation responsibilities.

## Who else makes decisions

Community publishers remain responsible for their notices and editorial choices. Council and authorised agencies control their own official information and updates.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Community publishers, neighbourhood organisations, council communications/disaster teams, local businesses and venue hosts.

## How related interests would be handled

Publishing, moderation and venue access would operate under transparent terms. Any council purchase of Luke's website or noticeboard systems would require independent handling.
