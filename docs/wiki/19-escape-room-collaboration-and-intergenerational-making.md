# 19 Escape-room collaboration and intergenerational making

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Learning, work and leadership

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

People of different generations could solve puzzles and make things together, building teamwork, trusted relationships and practical learning opportunities for young people.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Cooperative puzzles and making challenges could be adapted for libraries, schools and community centres. Participants who chose to do so could record a spoken reflection afterwards, helping them and the facilitators explore what they learned about teamwork.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Escape Room Team Building](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/escape-room-team-building/)**. Business-development research for an Australian escape-room network, with venue and market evidence, financial calculators, experience design, tourism and costs. Recorded learning, paid work and ongoing renewal form part of the network design.

- **[Infinity Creative Workshop](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/infinity-creative-workshop/)**. A training website for music videos, educational films, shorts, Blender and DaVinci Resolve, with practical exercises, design choices, provider comparisons and cost tools. It also develops Queens mentorship applications for creative and learning work.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

Council could consider suitable venues and youth, library or arts partnerships for collaborative escape-room sessions and intergenerational making, with community input into the activities.

## Who else makes decisions

Hosts and facilitators manage sessions, recordings and participant permissions; schools and community organisations choose their own involvement.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Schools, youth services, libraries, community venues, mentors, makers and participants across generations.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's game design, recording tools or delivery role would need independent consideration if council commissioned sessions, licensed the format or funded a host involving him.
