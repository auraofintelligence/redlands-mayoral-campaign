# 29 Preparedness and recovery coordination

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Disaster readiness and recovery

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Communities could strengthen their preparations for outages, disrupted transport and heat, and make support easier to coordinate during recovery.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A map of existing equipment, facilities, services and gaps could support household preparedness assistance agreed with each household, practice exercises and clear records of recovery tasks and available resources.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Aura Disaster Resilience](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-disaster-resilience/)**. A community-capability and disaster-resilience design program covering all-weather kiosks, local information, personal Aura, power, communications, preparedness and continuing recovery. Its wiki and source library organise the questions, design options, evidence and decisions.

- **[Straddie Capsule Surge Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-capsule-surge-lab/)**. A site-neutral inland capsule-hotel concept combining accommodation, health-surge logistics, civic simulation and shared computing on Minjerribah. It includes value and funding scenarios, compute questions and local Markdown planning builders.

- **[Aura for Adaptive Whole of Life Support](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-whole-of-life/)**. An Aura of Intelligence support proposal for adults living with dementia or disability, people beginning aged care, and their supporters and providers. Its reading site covers personal identity, memory, choices, relationships, support and connected research pathways.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

Existing Local Disaster Management Group and recovery arrangements provide the route for strengthening preparedness, information and handovers.

## Who else makes decisions

Other agencies remain responsible for their services and any protected health or support information.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Local Disaster Management Group and recovery partners, existing service providers, community organisations, utilities and households choosing to participate.

## How related interests would be handled

Community participation and support records would remain within arrangements agreed with the people involved. Any council recovery contract involving Luke or an organisation he joined would require independent assessment.
