# 23 Tip Loop repair and reuse

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Food, repair and community wealth

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Tip Loop aims to keep useful goods in circulation through collection, repair and affordable distribution, reducing replacement costs and waste.

## A practical example

A repairable appliance could be collected before disposal, checked by the appropriate repairer and made available at an affordable cost.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A safe collection route before disposal would need sorting, storage, checks on what can be repaired and affordable distribution. Interested sheds, makers and existing reuse operators could help develop the arrangements.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Straddie Tip Loop Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-tip-loop-lab/)**. A local materials-recovery and recycling planning lab for Minjerribah. It explores material streams, pre-disposal recovery, reuse, repair, microfactory tools and governance for keeping useful material and practical skills on the island.

- **[Straddie Maker-Space Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-makerspace-lab/)**. A proposal for a light-industrial maker-space in Dunwich / Goompi. It explores useful making, repair and right-to-repair, shared tools, compact workshop design, recycling, upcycling and local material knowledge.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could bring council waste teams, existing reuse operators, sheds and repairers together around pre-disposal collection, space, storage and distribution arrangements.

## Who else makes decisions

Waste-site operators, goods owners and repair/distribution hosts retain their operational roles. Specialist repair work belongs with appropriately qualified providers.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Council waste team and existing operators, men’s sheds, makers, licensed repairers and community distribution partners.

## How related interests would be handled

Access to council waste streams, land, grants or contracts would need independent assessment where Luke or a future co-operative had an interest. Allocation and redistribution arrangements would be published.
