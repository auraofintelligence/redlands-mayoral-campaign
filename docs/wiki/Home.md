## The shared aim: Joyful Responsible Abundance

Joyful Responsible Abundance is the shared aim: more room to live well, take part, learn and create, with care for other people, Country and the living world. The practical questions are how to ease avoidable costs, share useful resources, recognise contribution and give people a meaningful say in decisions that affect them.

- **Joyful**: Make room for connection, creativity, learning, enjoyment and a life beyond administration.
- **Responsible**: Respect choice, privacy and cultural authority; explain decisions and use resources with care.
- **Abundance**: Help more people gain access to useful skills, tools, services and opportunities, and share in the benefits.

[Read the introduction to Joyful Responsible Abundance](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/joyful-responsible-abundance/index.html)

## Words and names

These explanations describe how the terms are used in this Wiki. The linked project pages provide their own detail and sources.

### Artificial intelligence (AI)

Computer systems that can generate or analyse material such as text, images and data. Their output needs checking against the purpose and the source.

### Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura

Luke’s design for personal artificial intelligence (AI), working with a person’s chosen records to support learning, reflection and everyday tasks. The person chooses the purposes, permissions and what to share.

### Community Hours (C-hours)

A way to recognise verified voluntary human contribution and its outcomes. The source introduction describes one verified hour of contribution with no monetary equivalent. Participation and information sharing are choices.

### Genesis

Luke’s proposed series of sixty sessions for personal reflection, health, learning and developing a personal Aura. The participant’s own records and choices connect the process with everyday life.

### Verse Box

A proposed portable public station where people could try personal Aura, learning and community activities with support from a host. The trial guide covers equipment and clear handling of personal records.

### Geode

The proposed physical equipment and experience settings for Genesis, personal Aura and learning. The source guide describes several HBOT and Geode hardware paths.

### Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT)

Breathing oxygen in a pressurised chamber. Clinical services and proposed research have their own providers, participation choices and responsibilities.

### Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative (Ready SET Co-op)

A proposed member-governed organisation for shared work, assets, administration and services. Its members would agree its rules and responsibilities.

### Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth (GAJRA Earth)

Luke’s proposed network connecting contribution, learning, events, travel, research and shared capability. A Redlands organisation would have its own members and agreed rules.

### P4A

Luke’s public civic and political proposal. The leadership idea in this Wiki concerns the voluntary standards he chooses for himself.

### Sovereign capacity / sovereign compute

The practical ability to provide and govern useful computing, skills, equipment and services, including decisions about where data is kept and how systems are run.

### The missing middle

The people, places, equipment and ongoing work needed between an idea and a service people can actually use. The linked project develops a plan for locally owned community computing.

### Digital twin

A digital model of a real place, person or system. The purpose, information and permissions vary with what is being modelled.

### Data sovereignty

Who has authority over data, including where it is kept, what it may be used for and who can access it. Cultural knowledge has its own custodians and permissions.

### A node and a federation

A node is a local service within a wider network. A federation is an arrangement for those local services to work together while keeping agreed local authority.

### Governance

The rules for who decides, how people take part and how decisions can be checked.

### Procurement and grant acquittal

Procurement is the process of buying goods or services. A grant acquittal is a report showing how grant money was used and whether the agreed work was done.

### Intellectual property

Rights associated with work such as writing, designs and software. Ownership and permission to use the work need to be clear.

### Identity checks (KYC)

KYC stands for know your customer: a process for checking identity. Any use in a proposed service would need a clear purpose, suitable privacy arrangements and a lawful basis.
