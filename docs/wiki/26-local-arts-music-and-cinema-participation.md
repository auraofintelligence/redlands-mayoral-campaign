# 26 Local arts, music and cinema participation

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Public spaces, arts and local communication

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Affordable local arts, music and cinema activities could give people more ways to learn, create, enjoy themselves and connect with others.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Willing mainland and island venues could host music, film, making and cultural events. Organisers would agree accessible participation, rights to use the work and the authority of the relevant cultural custodians.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[i C. infinity Music Universe](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/i-C-infinity-music-universe/)**. The i C. infinity artist catalogue and creative studio, bringing together albums, songs, lyrics, video and their wider narrative worlds. Its studio tools support listening, storyboards, video briefs, production shots, distribution planning and handoffs.

- **[Quandamooka Film Festival](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/quandamooka-film-festival/)**. A festival-planning doorway and film-making toolkit for first-time, smartphone, documentary and AI-assisted creators. It provides story, asset-sharing, storyboard, screening and submission-planning builders.

- **[Film Club Documentary Builders](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/film-club-documentary-builders/)**. A documentary-planning toolkit that turns research, source trails, interview preparation, scene analysis, run sheets and screening notes into portable Markdown files. The forms save drafts in the browser and export them for the creator’s chosen workflow.

- **[Minjerribah Screen & Media Network](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/minjerribah-screen-media-network/)**. A community pitch for local stories, film, news, training and public information on Minjerribah. It describes how material could move through verification, editing, publication, screens and archiving, with local media-making as a learning activity.

- **[Straddie Night Market Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-night-market-lab/)**. A market-onboarding workbench for Straddie stallholders, artists, food providers, venues, organisers and helpers. It collects offers and site requirements, separates private follow-up from public listings, and includes transport and visitor-planning information.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

Council could support access to its arts venues, libraries, events and relevant community funding pathways for local music, cinema and creative participation.

## Who else makes decisions

Artists, venues and event organisers choose their programs and participation arrangements; content owners retain control of licensing and use.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Community groups, co-operatives, schools, businesses and external funders as appropriate.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's music, creative work or production role would need independent consideration if council funded an event, licensed content or commissioned work involving him.
