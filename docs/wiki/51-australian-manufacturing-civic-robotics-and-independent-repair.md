# 51 Australian manufacturing, civic robotics and independent repair

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Redlands sovereign capacity and civic establishments

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Redlands public-service and business needs could connect with Australian design, manufacture, maintenance and ownership of useful machines and tools, for local use and export.

## Words and project names

- **Artificial intelligence (AI)**: Computer systems that can generate or analyse material such as text, images and data. Their output needs checking against the purpose and the source.
- **Sovereign capacity / sovereign compute**: The practical ability to provide and govern useful computing, skills, equipment and services, including decisions about where data is kept and how systems are run.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A Redlands industry brief could set out equipment needs for water and stormwater, inspections, solar and land care, food growing, public-space maintenance, moving materials and emergency supplies. Willing businesses, workshops, manufacturers and training providers could work together on design, supply, repair and service. Exports could include machines, components, repair modules and service knowledge, on terms that preserve the buyer’s authority.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Sovereign Capacity Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/sovereign-capacity-lab/)**. A browser-based research and scenario laboratory for mining, materials, power, repairable robots and locally governed infrastructure. Its workbenches connect physical resource accounts, distributed compute, manufacturing, Australian industry and a 2032 planning horizon.

- **[Straddie Maker-Space Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-makerspace-lab/)**. A proposal for a light-industrial maker-space in Dunwich / Goompi. It explores useful making, repair and right-to-repair, shared tools, compact workshop design, recycling, upcycling and local material knowledge.

- **[Straddie Tip Loop Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/straddie-tip-loop-lab/)**. A local materials-recovery and recycling planning lab for Minjerribah. It explores material streams, pre-disposal recovery, reuse, repair, microfactory tools and governance for keeping useful material and practical skills on the island.

- **[Ready SET Co-op Trust Hub](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ready-set-co-op-trust-hub/)**. A trust-building, co-working and asset-sharing pathway toward Ready S.E.T. Co-op on Minjerribah. It explores local support work, training and paid roles, grant preparation and a possible community base at 9 Ballow Road.

- **[Luke’s World of Work](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Lukes-world-of-work-experience/)**. An illustrated harbour town built from Luke Hayes's work, education and volunteering history. Buildings and vehicles open individual source-backed records within one continuous fictional map.

## What it needs to work

Count machines made each year separately from the machines still working after older ones retire. Record both factory capacity and actual demand. Bring together parts, factories, installation and testing, skills, suitable buyers and local service support. Keep setup costs, money needed for day-to-day production, sales and profit in separate accounts. Designs would include documented connections between parts and systems, repair access, owner control, continuing software support and safe ways to isolate or disconnect equipment.

## How this could move forward

Council, regional, state, federal and business cooperation

## What the mayor and council could do

Council can articulate public-service needs, convene industry and workforce partners, support local capability and undertake authorised purchasing or partnerships. Neighbouring councils can choose to collaborate on demand, skills, service infrastructure and proposals to state and federal government.

## Who else makes decisions

Manufacturers and customers decide commercial production and sales. State/federal industry, trade and infrastructure settings and relevant safety authorities retain their responsibilities. National factory or export scenarios in the manuscript are distinct from Redlands orders or local production commitments.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Manufacturers and component suppliers, independent repairers, makers, employers and unions, education/training providers, researchers, service owners, neighbouring councils and state and federal industry and investment partners.

## How related interests would be handled

Relevant interests include ownership, intellectual-property income or a paid role in a manufacturer, equipment supplier or repair contract connected to a council decision.
