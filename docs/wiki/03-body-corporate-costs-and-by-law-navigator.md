# 03 Body corporate costs and by-law navigator

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Household savings and fairness

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Owners and occupants could use a body corporate navigator to understand levies and by-laws, compare costs and find the right way to raise a concern or resolve a dispute.

## What this could mean for Redlands

A checklist for each body corporate scheme could cover levies, budgets, insurance, maintenance, meeting rights, records requests and disputed by-laws. It would explain which Queensland rules apply and where professional help is available.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Australian Legal Engine: Read the engine guide](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-law-2012-lukes-relevance/)**. Offline software for parsing, indexing and searching Australian Acts held on the user’s own machine. It retrieves exact provisions with addresses and source dates, traces legislative cross-references and prepares cited handoffs for further research.

- **[Australian Household Bill Check](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/household-bill-check/)**. A workbench for investigating Australian household contracts and fees, annualising charges and preparing provider requests or legal-research briefs. It also gathers consented evidence summaries for reform ideas and a Straddie Mutuals collective-bargaining thought experiment.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could bring owners, occupants and advice providers together and seek information and referral support through council’s community functions.

## Who else makes decisions

The relevant body corporate scheme and Queensland law govern by-laws and levies. The Office of the Commissioner for Body Corporate and Community Management (BCCM) provides information and dispute services within its role.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Owners and occupants, bodies corporate, managers, strata lawyers, BCCM and consumer advisers.

## How related interests would be handled

A general information service would be separate from any council transaction involving Luke’s software or business. Each participating scheme would choose its own advisers and purchasing arrangements.
