# 04 AI and superintelligence readiness

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · AI capability and accountable council

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Council could prepare for advancing artificial intelligence (AI) through useful staff support, reliable public services and human responsibility for decisions.

## Words and project names

- **Artificial intelligence (AI)**: Computer systems that can generate or analyse material such as text, images and data. Their output needs checking against the purpose and the source.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Council could develop an AI policy and demonstrate improvements to everyday services. The work would cover records, access to data, staff support, purchasing, protection against online attacks, keeping services running and human review. Different services could draw on shared computing and support while keeping their own responsibilities.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Sovereign Capacity Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/sovereign-capacity-lab/)**. A browser-based research and scenario laboratory for mining, materials, power, repairable robots and locally governed infrastructure. Its workbenches connect physical resource accounts, distributed compute, manufacturing, Australian industry and a 2032 planning horizon.

- **[Joyful Responsible Abundance](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/joyful-responsible-abundance/)**. An educational website introducing Luke's whole-system proposal and his paper on superintelligence and Australian sovereignty. Reading paths, source documents and calculators connect people, productive capacity, civic institutions and wider world questions.

- **[Installing the Missing Middle](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/installing-the-missing-middle/)**. A national installation-business plan for a federation of locally owned community-compute nodes. It combines computing, useful heat, backup power, installation learning, shared ownership, finance and hardware renewal.

- **[A Fair Go in the Age of Intelligence](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/UNGA81-Luke-Hayes/)**. Luke’s independent UNGA81 contribution, A Fair Go in the Age of Intelligence. It connects Joyful Responsible Abundance with shared capability, livelihoods and time, personal and civic intelligence, and Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth (GAJRA Earth).

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could build support for council’s AI policy and service objectives, with implementation, staff involvement and reporting managed through the CEO.

## Who else makes decisions

Service owners, the CEO and authorised officers retain their operational and decision responsibilities; relevant external laws, contracts and regulators still apply.

## People and organisations who could be involved

CEO and council service leaders, workforce representatives, neighbouring councils, researchers and technology businesses.

## How related interests would be handled

Any council purchase, licence or service arrangement connected to Luke’s AI software, training, support or financial interests would be assessed as a specific transaction under the applicable interest rules.
