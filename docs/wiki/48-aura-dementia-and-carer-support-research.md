# 48 Aura dementia and carer-support research

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Health, care and inclusion

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Personal Aura tools could help people with dementia and their carers preserve familiar memories, express choices and maintain relationships, with research into how well the tools work.

## Words and project names

- **Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura**: Luke’s approach to personal AI, built around a person’s chosen records, purposes, permissions and relationship with the system.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Willing people with dementia, carers and care and research partners could develop familiar music and images, personal records, reminders and support for everyday choices. Research would examine ease of use, quality of life, everyday abilities and outcomes for carers.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Aura Dementia - concept and research pathway](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-dementia/)**. A design and research site exploring a privacy-first digital companion for people living with dementia, their families and caregivers. Its pages cover memory, identity, connection, technology, family experiences and a proposed research pathway.

- **[Aura for Adaptive Whole of Life Support](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-whole-of-life/)**. An Aura of Intelligence support proposal for adults living with dementia or disability, people beginning aged care, and their supporters and providers. Its reading site covers personal identity, memory, choices, relationships, support and connected research pathways.

- **[82 Claytons Road: A Gift for Generations](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Anglican-Diocese-82-Claytons-Amity/)**. A land-gift and partnership invitation for 82 Claytons Road, Amity, prepared for Anglican Church Southern Queensland. The proposal brings together dementia support, aged care, longevity research, learning and community participation, with its manuscript and document library.

## How this could move forward

Care and research partnerships

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could convene local care and community partners, connect accessible public learning and age-friendly work, and support a funding conversation with the responsible governments and institutions.

## Who else makes decisions

Care delivery, clinical research, consent arrangements and health information remain with the responsible participants, providers and research bodies.

## People and organisations who could be involved

People with dementia, carers, aged-care and home-care providers, clinicians, researchers, community organisations and suitable technology partners.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's dementia-support software, research role or provider interest would be relevant to any council study funding, software licence or selection of a research partner involving him.
