# 14 Luke’s personal HBOT purchase and Genesis journey

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Personal Genesis and leadership

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Luke proposes to develop his own Aura of Intelligence through a personal Genesis journey. The optional adult version combines hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) with added healing intent and time-stamped biometric records for further pattern recognition. The Truth Engine is an additional optional element.

## Words and project names

- **Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura**: Luke’s design for personal artificial intelligence (AI), working with a person’s chosen records to support learning, reflection and everyday tasks. The person chooses the purposes, permissions and what to share.
- **Genesis**: Luke’s proposed series of sixty sessions for personal reflection, health, learning and developing a personal Aura. The participant’s own records and choices connect the process with everyday life.
- **Verse Box**: A proposed portable public station where people could try personal Aura, learning and community activities with support from a host. The trial guide covers equipment and clear handling of personal records.
- **Geode**: The proposed physical equipment and experience settings for Genesis, personal Aura and learning. The source guide describes several HBOT and Geode hardware paths.
- **Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT)**: Breathing oxygen in a pressurised chamber. Clinical services and proposed research have their own providers, participation choices and responsibilities.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Luke proposes to buy his own HBOT equipment and establish his first Genesis journey. HBOT involves breathing oxygen in a pressurised chamber. The design adds a healing intent and records biometric observations with time stamps so they can be examined alongside reflection and personal Aura development for further patterns. Luke would choose his records, permissions, everyday uses and whether to include the optional Truth Engine. This adult pathway is a separate choice from beginning an Aura through Verse Box.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Aura Genesis](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis/)**. A website presenting the Aura Genesis journey as sixty sessions combining reflection, health, personal digital twins and community participation. It follows a staged player journey within an open-ended infinite-game framework.

- **[Aura Genesis Protocol](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis-geode-atlas/)**. A guide to the Aura Genesis person-Aura development pathway, Truth Engine research, sixty-day teacher development and four HBOT/Geode hardware pathways. It includes course readings, materials research and experience-environment design.

- **[Verse Box](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-verse-box/)**. A portable public introduction to personal Aura of Intelligence, learning, Community Hours and community participation. The trial guide covers the visitor experience, equipment, hosts, access and personal-session handover.

- **[i C. infinity Music Universe](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/i-C-infinity-music-universe/)**. The i C. infinity artist catalogue and creative studio, bringing together albums, songs, lyrics, video and their wider narrative worlds. Its studio tools support listening, storyboards, video briefs, production shots, distribution planning and handoffs.

## How this could move forward

Personal choice, with separate decisions for any council involvement

## What the mayor and council could do

Luke’s voluntary participation could provide a personal example for conversations about human agency, learning and personal intelligence. Any council program or partnership would have its own decision route.

## Who else makes decisions

Luke would decide his participation, equipment purchase, private records and chosen disclosures. Suppliers, providers and venue owners would retain their own responsibilities.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Equipment and software providers, venue and operating partners chosen by Luke, and people he invites into his learning.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke’s personally purchased HBOT equipment and Genesis intellectual property would be relevant interests if council funded, hosted, purchased or arranged access to them.
