# 18 Try Everything work exploration

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Learning, work and leadership

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Try Everything work exploration could help people discover suitable work through direct experience, local connections and opportunities to learn new skills.

## Words and project names

- **Community Hours (C-hours)**: A way to recognise verified voluntary human contribution and its outcomes. The source introduction describes one verified hour of contribution with no monetary equivalent. Participation and information sharing are choices.
- **Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative (Ready SET Co-op)**: A proposed member-governed organisation for shared work, assets, administration and services. Its members would agree its rules and responsibilities.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Short experiences in trades, care, arts, technology, food growing and repair could help people discover work that suits them. Schools, employment services and employers would use recognised placement arrangements and connect interested people with paid training or employment opportunities.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Luke’s World of Work](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Lukes-world-of-work-experience/)**. An illustrated harbour town built from Luke Hayes's work, education and volunteering history. Buildings and vehicles open individual source-backed records within one continuous fictional map.

- **[Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative (Ready SET Co-op) Trust Hub](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ready-set-co-op-trust-hub/)**. A public concept for Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative (Ready SET Co-op) covering co-working, shared equipment, training, local media and trust-building. Its 9 Ballow Road proposal at Dunwich / Gumpi concerns a building Luke identifies as still vacant. The Trust Hub has not been established.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

Council can convene employers, offer appropriate opportunities and support school/employment partnerships.

## Who else makes decisions

Schools, employment providers and employers hold their own placement, employment, training and safeguarding responsibilities.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Schools, employers, employment agencies, TAFE and other training providers and community hosts.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke's work-exploration tools or host role would need independent consideration if council funded placements, bought program support or selected a delivery partner involving him.
