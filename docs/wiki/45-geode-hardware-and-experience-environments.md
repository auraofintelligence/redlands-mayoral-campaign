# 45 Geode hardware and experience environments

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Infrastructure, energy and coasts

## Purpose

Develop the physical environments and interfaces supporting Genesis, personal Aura and immersive learning.

## Possible Redlands action

Develop the Geode hardware paths, including integration with seated HBOT, mineral/geopolymer design, crystalline environments and their connections to compute, sensing and accessible interfaces. Keep each hardware path open to its own collaborators and design decisions.

## Decision route

Geode hardware and experience environments

## Mayoral and council lever

Convene manufacturers, researchers and education partners; connect relevant local skills, making, economic-development and funding opportunities through council’s normal processes.

## Other decision-makers

Design, manufacture, equipment operation and any required approvals sit with the responsible specialists, providers and regulators.

## Partners

Manufacturers, chamber and interface providers, materials researchers, engineers, makers, universities and accessibility specialists.

## Specific interest or transaction boundary

Your Geode designs, hardware, software or supplier role are relevant if council commissions prototypes, purchases equipment or funds development involving you.
