# 06 Easier ways to deal with council

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · AI capability and accountable council

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Residents could spend less time making repeat calls, fixing incomplete applications or travelling to resolve routine council matters.

## Words and project names

- **Artificial intelligence (AI)**: Computer systems that can generate or analyse material such as text, images and data. Their output needs checking against the purpose and the source.

## A practical example

A resident could see which documents an application needs and what happens next, instead of retelling the same story at each step.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Councillors, residents and staff could identify forms and processes that repeatedly cause difficulty. Clear guidance, help with forms, accessible progress updates and a checked source of council information could then be trialled.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[Agent Markdown Form Navigator](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/agent-md-form-navigator/)**. A searchable directory of project-family pages that collect structured inputs and produce agent-ready Markdown. Records identify each page's outputs, scripts, hosting route and connections to other pages.

- **[Aura Civic Establishment](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-civic-establishment/)**. A shared civic-place proposal exploring a round-the-clock mix of activities, rooms, public access, KYC, computing and operating arrangements. It includes overlap tools, capacity calculators and a Brisbane candidate-site study.

- **[Fishing Club AI Helper Forms](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/plfc-ai-agent-md-forms/)**. A set of Point Lookout Fishing Club forms that generate AI-ready Markdown files. The forms cover meetings, members and volunteers, events, social content, source notes, grants, education and visitors.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could seek service improvements and a council decision on the experience residents should receive. The CEO would manage integration with existing systems.

## Who else makes decisions

The CEO and responsible officers manage systems and operational decisions within their authority. Integration must account for existing contracts, information access, privacy and records obligations.

## People and organisations who could be involved

CEO, customer service and digital teams, residents, accessibility users and existing/new providers through the proper process.

## How related interests would be handled

Any council purchase, licence or integration involving Luke’s software or Strange But True would require independent assessment and purchasing. Resident case information would remain within the authorised service workflow.
