# 05 Free and low-cost AI and data sovereignty learning

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · AI capability and accountable council

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

Luke Nathan Hayes decided on 10 November 2026 not to contest the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. This Wiki explores civic questions and possible project adaptations.

## The idea

Free and low-cost learning could help residents, community groups and small businesses use AI confidently and make informed choices about their data.

## Words and project names

- **Artificial intelligence (AI)**: Computer systems that can generate or analyse material such as text, images and data. Their output needs checking against the purpose and the source.
- **Verse Box**: A proposed portable public station where people could try personal Aura, learning and community activities with support from a host. The trial guide covers equipment and clear handling of personal records.
- **Data sovereignty**: Who has authority over data, including where it is kept, what it may be used for and who can access it. Cultural knowledge has its own custodians and permissions.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Accessible online lessons and optional supported sessions could cover useful AI instructions, checking sources, spotting scams, keeping documents private, backups, moving data between services, running AI on local equipment and choosing providers. Suitable free official training could be included.

## Projects behind this idea

These introductions describe the source projects. The Redlands proposal above explains the possible local adaptation.

- **[How to Use Markdown with AI](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/how-to-use-md-with-ai/)**. A guide and browser-local builder for preparing portable Markdown context before working with AI. It explains personal profiles, Aura context, public records, source trails and separation of private material from public information.

- **[Aura Teacher Training](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-teacher-training/)**. A sixty-day adult learning progression connecting personal Aura development, self-understanding, critical and ethical AI use, and teaching. Daily readings and studio guides include practical making, teach-back, evidence and learning portfolios.

- **[Strange but True](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/strange-but-true/)**. Luke's Minjerribah public doorway for practical technology and AI help, event and media support, grants, civic ideas, creative downloads and the Community Ledger. It keeps paid-work trails and voluntary C-Hours as separate pathways.

## How this could move forward

Council services and policy decisions

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could seek council support for accessible online resources, library and community learning, and partnerships with education and digital-inclusion providers.

## Who else makes decisions

Independent providers choose their teaching offers and delivery arrangements; formal qualifications remain with the responsible education bodies.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Libraries, TAFE and other education providers, community centres, small-business networks and digital inclusion organisations.

## How related interests would be handled

If council funded teaching, licensed Luke’s learning materials or purchased support from Strange But True, the related interest would be identified and handled through the applicable decision process.
