# 08 Voluntary C-hour demonstration

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · [Words and names](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home#words-and-names) · Community contribution and reciprocity

An idea by [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/redlands-mayoral-campaign/about.html), an Amity resident on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island working on digital tools, community proposals and music.

## The idea

Voluntary Community Hours (C-hours) could recognise useful contributions, support skill sharing and help people organise community work, with participants choosing what they share.

## Words and project names

- **Aura of Intelligence / personal Aura**: Luke’s design for personal artificial intelligence (AI), working with a person’s chosen records to support learning, reflection and everyday tasks. The person chooses the purposes, permissions and what to share.
- **Community Hours (C-hours)**: A way to recognise verified voluntary human contribution and its outcomes. The source introduction describes one verified hour of contribution with no monetary equivalent. Participation and information sharing are choices.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Willing hosts could develop contribution opportunities in care, repairs, gardens, digital help, arts and preparedness. Following the September 2026 C-hour direction, records would connect the activity and its results with freely given time, confirmation of the contribution and reflection on what was learned. Each participant would choose what personal information to share.

## How this could move forward

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could bring willing hosts together, support community discussion of verification and participation, and seek council consideration of venues or support for a voluntary C-hour demonstration.

## Who else makes decisions

Participants choose whether and how to contribute; hosts manage activities and records under their own arrangements, and funding bodies decide applications addressed to them.

## People and organisations who could be involved

Willing community hosts, residents, carers, volunteer organisations and businesses offering appropriate support.

## How related interests would be handled

Luke’s C-hour tools, intellectual property or delivery role would be relevant interests if council funded, hosted or commissioned a contribution program involving them.
