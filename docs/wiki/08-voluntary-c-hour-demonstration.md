# 08 Voluntary C-hour demonstration

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Community contribution and reciprocity

## Purpose

Recognise useful contributions and make it easier to exchange skills and organise community work.

## Possible Redlands action

Develop the C-hour contribution journey with willing hosts across care, repairs, gardens, digital help, arts and preparedness. Record the actual activity and outcome alongside freely given time, verification, reflection and chosen information sharing, using the September 2026 C-hour direction.

## Decision route

Community partnership

## Mayoral and council lever

Bring willing contribution hosts together, support community discussion of verification and participation, and seek council consideration of venues or support for a voluntary C-hour demonstration.

## Other decision-makers

Participants choose whether and how to contribute; hosts manage activities and records under their own arrangements, and funding bodies decide applications addressed to them.

## Partners

Willing community hosts, residents, carers, volunteer organisations and businesses offering appropriate support.

## Specific interest or transaction boundary

Your C-hour tools, IP or delivery role are relevant if council funds, hosts or commissions the contribution programme.
