# 08 Voluntary C-hour demonstration

[Wiki home](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/redlands-mayoral-campaign/wiki/Home) · Community contribution and reciprocity

## Purpose

Voluntary C-hours could recognise useful contributions, help people exchange skills and make community work easier to organise.

## What this could mean for Redlands

Willing hosts could develop contribution opportunities across care, repairs, gardens, digital help, arts and preparedness. Following the September 2026 C-hour direction, records would connect actual activities and outcomes with freely given time, verification, reflection and each participant’s choices about information sharing.

## Decision route

Community partnership

## What the mayor and council could do

The mayor could bring willing hosts together, support community discussion of verification and participation, and seek council consideration of venues or support for a voluntary C-hour demonstration.

## Other decision-makers

Participants choose whether and how to contribute; hosts manage activities and records under their own arrangements, and funding bodies decide applications addressed to them.

## Possible partners

Willing community hosts, residents, carers, volunteer organisations and businesses offering appropriate support.

## Interests and independent decisions

Luke’s C-hour tools, intellectual property or delivery role would be relevant interests if council funded, hosted or commissioned a contribution programme involving them.
