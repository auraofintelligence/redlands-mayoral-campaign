Luke Nathan Hayes: media facts

Name: Luke Nathan Hayes
Base: Amity, Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island, Queensland
Artist name: i C. infinity

Luke's work spans practical technology, personal intelligence, community systems, public submissions and creative production.

Luke is not contesting the 2026 Redland City mayoral by-election. His current work is an independent Redlands civic simulation and an invitation to explore projects, collaborate and discuss licensing and advisory. Luke is the sole human operator, supported by an AI team. His approach remains relaxed and self-directed.
