MUSIC DURING THE CAMPAIGN
Luke Nathan Hayes / i C. infinity
Prepared 10 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane

Music, ideas and candidacy

Luke writes the lyrics for i C. infinity and uses generative AI to create the music. He has four completed albums and does not currently perform them live. A Protopian Gambit contains 22 songs across EDM and house, pop, rock, cultural, reggae and coastal styles. Don't Try to Fix Me contains severe language and is an angry song about big tech.

The songs carry ideas from Luke's wider body of work. Some of those projects also appear on his public Redlands mayoral campaign website. The music has a commercial catalogue as well as a place in his public discussion of community ideas and a possible candidacy. These connections should be clear to anyone considering a broadcast or interview.

Editorial decisions and airtime arrangements

Stations and presenters make their own programming and interview decisions. Supplying music or accepting an interview does not grant Luke editorial control or establish that an outlet endorses him. No airplay, interview, booking or endorsement is asserted in this pack.

Please make clear whether an offer is independently chosen programming, a guest interview, advertising, sponsorship or another arrangement. Any payment, exchange, discount or offered campaign support should be stated separately. An editorial broadcast is not automatically entered as a campaign donation or assigned an advertising value. A record of a broadcast does not itself settle its legal classification.

Please help keep a complete broadcast record

Luke wants a record of every known song play and appearance during the campaign as part of his ethical overcompliance approach. Please provide a playlist, transmission log or confirmation after broadcast, where available, covering:

- Station or outlet and programme or presenter.
- Song title and version or edit, including full song or excerpt.
- Broadcast date, start time and time zone. State whether the time is exact or approximate. If a time is unknown, leave it unknown rather than estimating it as fact.
- Duration, if known.
- Radio, simultaneous radio/online stream, online-only stream, podcast or other platform.
- Every repeat or later replay, with its own date and time.
- A public playlist, replay link, recording timestamp or private transmission-log reference, where available.
- The actual airtime arrangement and any related payment or exchange, separately from the track information.

For an album feature, please list each track actually played, not only the feature's start time. Interviews should have separate entries. A simultaneous radio and online broadcast can be identified as a simulcast; a later replay is a separate occurrence.

Scheduled appearances, listener reports and confirmed broadcasts are kept distinct. Complete coverage depends on station records and confirmed reports. No automatic monitoring of every station or guaranteed completeness is claimed. Corrections and additional logs are welcome.
