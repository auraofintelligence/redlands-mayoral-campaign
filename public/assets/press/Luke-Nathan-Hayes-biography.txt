Luke Nathan Hayes

Luke Nathan Hayes is based at Amity on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island. His public collection brings together practical technology, personal intelligence, community systems, public submissions, music and ideas about long-term civilisation planning.

His working history spans retail, automotive servicing and wheel alignment, festival and event work, railway training, airport services, mine shutdowns, driving and web work. His illustrated World of Work links individual records to the underlying work, education and volunteering history.

Strange But True is a doorway into his practical and creative work. Project Atlas helps readers explore the wider public collection, while the Open Record connects life history, original documents and project sources.

Luke writes the lyrics for i C. infinity and uses generative AI to create the music. He has four completed albums. His latest, A Protopian Gambit, mixes EDM and house, pop, rock, cultural, reggae and coastal styles. He does not currently perform the music live.
