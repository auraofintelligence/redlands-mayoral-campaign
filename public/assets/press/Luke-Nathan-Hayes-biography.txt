Luke Nathan Hayes

Luke Nathan Hayes is based at Amity on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island. His public collection brings together practical technology, personal intelligence, community systems, public submissions, music and ideas about long-term civilisation planning.

Luke's work history spans retail, vehicle mechanics, festivals and events, construction, rail electrification, airport services, transport and logistics, sales, market research, web design, generative AI research and more. His published World of Work describes his individual roles, employers and tasks in Australia and the United Kingdom. See Luke-Nathan-Hayes-work-history.txt in this kit for the full list and direct record links.

Strange But True is a doorway into his practical and creative work. Project Atlas helps readers explore the wider public collection, while the Open Record connects life history, original documents and project sources.

As i C. infinity, Luke writes his lyrics and creates his music using generative AI. He has four completed albums. His latest, A Protopian Gambit, mixes EDM and house, pop, rock, cultural, reggae and coastal styles. He does not currently perform the music live.
