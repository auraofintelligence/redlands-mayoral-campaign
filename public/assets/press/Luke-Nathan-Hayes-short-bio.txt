Luke Nathan Hayes

Luke Nathan Hayes is based at Amity on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island. His work connects practical technology, personal intelligence, community systems, public submissions and creative production. As i C. infinity, he writes his lyrics and creates his music using generative AI.
